  • National Power Portal (CEA, Ministry of Power): station map, daily generation, daily generation report and daily coal report.
  • TNPGCL, TNGECL, TANTRANSCO; TN Environment Department, Database on Energy Resources in Tamil Nadu; TNGIS substation layer.
  • OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL): lines, substations, plant footprints, wind turbines; basemap via OpenFreeMap.
  • Sentinel-2 cloudless by EOX IT Services GmbH (contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2016, CC BY 4.0); terrain from AWS Terrain Tiles; weather from Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0).
  • India outline and states: DataMeet (CC BY 4.0). Built with MapLibre GL JS, three.js and pixelarticons; fonts Pixelify Sans and Silkscreen (OFL).

Station positions from the National Power Portal were checked against their state and against OpenStreetMap and the Tamil Nadu dossiers; copied or out-of-state points were corrected or left off (listed in the guide). Output shown per station is the previous day's average (energy ÷ 24 h), because stations' instantaneous output is not published; Tamil Nadu's statewide figures are the SLDC's instantaneous readings.
